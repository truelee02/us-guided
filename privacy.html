Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Us, Guided is a shared chat with an AI Guide for couples and families. Because the whole point of the app is talking about personal things, we keep this policy short, plain, and honest about exactly what happens to your information.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Your profile: the things you choose to enter — birthday, birth city and time (if you add them), love language and attachment quiz results, and what you write about how you communicate and what you're working on.
  • Your household's content: the messages posted to your shared chat, scheduled messages waiting to post, reminders, date ideas, daily focuses, and connection activities.
  • Nothing else. No advertising trackers, no analytics cookies, no location tracking. The only cookie we use keeps you signed in.

One thing we never store

When you write a message, the Guide may soften harsh wording before it posts. Your raw, unedited draft is never saved — only the version that actually posts to the chat is kept.

How the AI Guide works, and who else sees your words

To reword messages and give advice, the app sends your message, some recent chat context, and relevant parts of your household's profiles to Google's Gemini AI service, which generates the response. That means Google processes that content under its own API terms. We do not send your information to anyone else, and we never sell your data or show you ads.

Who can see your household's chat

Only the people in your household — the people who joined with your invite code. Your conversations are not public, not shared with other households, and not used to train our own models.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored on servers in the United States operated by our hosting provider (Render), and travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We keep automatic backup snapshots so a technical failure doesn't erase your household's history.

Your choices and controls

  • Edit or clear anything in your profile at any time.
  • Astrology is opt-in only — the Guide never uses it unless you turn it on.
  • Delete your account from the Us tab at any time. That permanently removes your profile, your messages, and your focuses and reminders. If yours is the only account in the household, the entire household is deleted.
  • Questions or a hand with any of this: email support@usguided.com.

Children

Us, Guided is for adults (18+). Please don't create accounts for children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top and note it in the app. Using the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
